What business problems does the Customer eXperience Tools app soft at Walmart

The Customer experience Tools at Walmart is the system that organizes every product into a structured hierarchy so it can searched, filtered, priced, and managed correctly across stores, online systems, and internal tools.

Walmart organizes products using hierarchical structure.


1. Department
Highest-level category
Example: Electronics, Grocery, Apparel, Home

2. Category
A sub-group under each department
Example (under Electronics): Computers, TVs, Cameras

3. Sub-Category
More specific grouping
Example (under Computers): Laptops, Desktops, Tablets

4. Item Type / Product Type
Defines what the item specifically is
This level affects how the product behaves in systems
Example (under Laptops): Gaming Laptop, Chromebook, 2-in-1 Laptop

5. Attributes
Each item type has required and optional attributes:
Color
Size
Weight
Brand
Nutritional details (for food)
Material (for apparel)



Walmart’s taxonomy determines:
✔ Search Ranking

Which items appear when customers search.
✔ Filters & Facets

What attributes (e.g., size, color, age range) show in search filters.
✔ Pricing Logic

Some categories have rules (e.g., MAP pricing for electronics).
✔ Compliance Requirements

Food, cosmetics, electronics all have different compliance metadata.
✔ Item Setup Requirements

Different categories require different mandatory attributes.
✔ Shelf Labeling & Inventory Behavior


The Customer eXperience Tools also has a item-condition-smart-dropdown feature 

Where merchants can choose the item Condition (New, Refurbished, Returned, New-without-tag, etc)
Eligibility rules (which items can be sold online or store-only)
Which widgets and dashboards to show specific product data
