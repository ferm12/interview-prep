Describe challenging project what was the outcome of your decision and what was so challenging about it.
  

One of the most challenging projects I led was the transition of our Unified Compute platform from JavaScript to TypeScript. The challenge wasn’t just technical—it was also about team alignment and 
Push back from some team members. Some team members were resistant to the shift, expressing concerns about losing familiarity with parts of the existing codebase and stepping out of their comfort zones, especially since not everyone had prior experience with TypeScript. Additionally, we were under significant time pressure, with only one quarter—about four months—to complete the migration and deliver a production-ready, bug-free release. I had to strike a balance between upskilling the team quickly and keeping up with our deadlines. Through clear communication, mentoring, and fostering collaboration, we adapted and successfully delivered the project on time, fully converted to TypeScript and with zero critical issues in production. The experience strengthened my leadership skills and showed the importance of aligning a team around a common technical goal.
